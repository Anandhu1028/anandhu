01 — Who I Am

Anandhu

I'm Anandhu, a Laravel full-stack developer based in Kochi, Kerala, working with training institutes, clinics, startups, and small-to-medium businesses that are outgrowing spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and disconnected tools.

My work starts with the business problem, not the code. Before I open an editor, I want to understand how leads move through your pipeline, where your team loses time, and what "done" actually looks like for the people using the system every day.

From there I design a data model and architecture that can grow with you — role-based access, clean APIs, and admin panels your staff can actually use — then build it in Laravel with the same care I'd want if it were my own business running on it.

Consultative, not just technical

I ask about your workflow before recommending a build, so the system matches how your team actually works.

Architecture built to last

Clean database design and modular Laravel code so adding new features later doesn't mean rebuilding from scratch.

Real-world business software

CRMs, ERPs, and dashboards that handle the messy edge cases of actual operations, not just the demo path.
